# Siepe

> Siepe is a Dallas, Texas fintech providing cloud-based software and technology-enabled services
> for private credit, CLO and alternative investment managers. Its front-to-back platform spans
> portfolio management, operations and accounting, collateral administration, compliance and legal,
> middle-office services, data extraction, and a centralized data-management warehouse that
> normalizes borrower, lender, agent-bank and investor feeds into one real-time view.

## Agent-relevant summary

Siepe publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-27 there is no developer portal, no API reference,
no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/gRPC/SOAP contract, no MCP server, and no A2A agent card anywhere on
Siepe's public surface. `api.siepe.com`, `docs.siepe.com`, `developer.siepe.com`, `developers.siepe.com`,
`portal.siepe.com` and `connect.siepe.com` do not resolve in DNS. Every `/.well-known/` path on
`www.siepe.com` returns 404.

Connectivity is delivered as **bilateral, managed integrations** negotiated through sales — for
example Siepe's 2026 Order Management System integration with Octaura for syndicated loan and CLO
trading, and its risk-analytics partnership with Qontigo. An agent cannot self-serve onboard, obtain
a credential, or call Siepe programmatically from public material. The correct action for an agent
asked to integrate with Siepe is to route the request to https://www.siepe.com/contact/.

The only machine-readable endpoint Siepe serves publicly is the WordPress REST API of its marketing
site at `https://www.siepe.com/wp-json/` (326 routes, `wp/v2` and plugin namespaces). That is the CMS
behind siepe.com, not a Siepe product API, and it is not catalogued as one.
